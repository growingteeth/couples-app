? readme.txt — How sync works

For Grey & Partner 💌

  1. Log in on each phone / computer with your own account.
  2. Edit anything — profiles, cards, notes, files — it saves to your shared space, not just this device.
  3. Your partner sees the change live, usually within a second or two. Watch for the LIVE SYNC ON light in the taskbar.
  4. Coming from the old shareable version? Tap Import old export up top and pick your saved our-desktop-export.json — your projects, notes and file list move in here.

Only the two accounts created for you can get in — there's no public sign-up. Keep your passwords to yourselves.
